## Technical preparation

- `scripts/immersion_assets_prepare.py` creates the small KayKit civilian prototypes from the pinned original GLBs.
- `scripts/immersion_assets_quaternius.py` creates the Quaternius runtime humans, garments and merged clips. Use the bundled Python 3.12 runtime with NumPy. Selected original source files and licenses are preserved in `assets/sources/quaternius/`; `IMPORT.json` fingerprints the complete official ZIPs. An optional `--base-archive PATH --animation-archive PATH` imports those two free Standard archives again.
- `scripts/immersion_assets_validate.py` independently evaluates glTF transforms and linear-blend skinning for both exported characters. `assets/characters/quaternius_validation.json` records five sampled poses per clip (90 poses total), bounds, joint/weight checks and output hashes. This is numeric validation; it does not certify clothing contact, animation quality or physical-device rendering.
- `scripts/immersion_assets_foliage.py` packages the three selected nature meshes from the free Standard archive. `assets/foliage/foliage.json` preserves source/output hashes, topology counts and scale notes.
- `scripts/immersion_assets_gltf_validate.cjs` runs the official local Khronos glTF validator. The current five character/foliage GLBs have zero errors. Character warnings concern the preserved identity Armature parent of skinned meshes; unused UVs are informational. The tool was installed only in a temporary directory; it is not a runtime dependency. Reports are in `assets/characters/khronos_validation.json`.
- Add `--environment` to validate the final district independently; its report is `assets/characters/environment_khronos_validation.json`. The frozen district has zero errors and 178 generated-tangent-space warnings: its normal maps rely on PlayCanvas to generate tangents during import. This warning concerns portability across rendering implementations; it is not a browser performance or appearance measurement.
- `scripts/immersion_assets_manifest.py` refreshes the complete asset inventory after rebuilds.
- The current model mapping is `assets/characters/characters.json`. `quaternius_characters.json` records the new mapping; `kaykit_characters.json` preserves the earlier spike mapping. Roles use actual named source clips rather than synthetic idle-bobbing or moving an unanimated marker.
- Scale is glTF meters, Y up, +Z facing. Per-model scale normalizes source body height to 1.7m; hair adds a small amount. Runtime material names `KALENDS_Cloth` and `KALENDS_Cloth_Trim` permit per-character wardrobe accents without recoloring skin.
- ARM maps use red = ambient occlusion, green = roughness, blue = metallic. Base color is sRGB; normal and ARM maps are linear. Individual tile widths are in `assets/materials/materials.json`.

## Reconstruction limits

The district and linen wardrobe are Roman-inspired interpretations, not archaeological reconstructions. The professional humanoid bases were not authored as named historical people. The animation library is general-purpose motion, not captured Roman baking or ancient gestures. Modern photographed PBR surfaces are material references rather than photographs of Pompeii. The fallback KayKit costumes remain stylized fantasy clothing and must not be described as authentic Roman dress.

No Mixamo files, paid Quaternius Pro/Source tiers, purchased models or external model inference were used. Blender was not installed or required by this preparation pipeline. Native browser inspection and gameplay validation are recorded in the main handoff separately from these asset checks.
