Kalends Immersion original environment mesh and procedural texture source

The architectural geometry, lettering layout and procedural texture patterns
generated by scripts/build_immersion_scene.py are original project-authored
assets. They have no third-party model source. Rights follow the project owner
and any future project release terms; no public-domain dedication or external
publication is made here.

The GLB embeds separately acquired CC0 Poly Haven materials. Their source,
author, original URLs and integrity fingerprints are recorded in:
../materials/materials.json and the project's asset provenance manifest.
Their authorship is not claimed by Kalends. The original JPG assets are retained
unmodified alongside that manifest. No asset purchase was made.

The scene is a stylized, schematic Roman-inspired reconstruction. It is not an
archaeological model, an authenticated reconstruction of particular ancient
buildings, or a source for surveyed dimensions. Attested place names and the
simulation's fictional residents do not authenticate the invented furnishings.
