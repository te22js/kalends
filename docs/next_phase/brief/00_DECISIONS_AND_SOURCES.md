# Kalends — decisions, evidence and scope

Prepared 24 October 2026. This is an execution brief, not a report of changes made to the repository.

## 1. Decision

**Keep Kalends' ambition broad; make its next proof narrow.** Build a human-facing entrance to two existing persistent worlds, one excellent return-to-your-history journey, and one properly discriminating study of memory-dependent decisions. Do not launch another engine, renderer, versioned “Mind” brand or speculative platform rewrite.

The product thesis is:

> Curated worlds in which your choices become a history that persists, other inhabitants act on their own information, and you control how that history is experienced.

The underlying runtime can eventually support entertainment, historical exploration, training and research. That does not mean the first page should advertise six businesses, or that one prototype establishes all six.

### What context recovery actually recovered

The August–September conversation retrieval consistently emphasizes the user's interest in inhabiting worlds rather than watching stories; authored worlds with unscripted consequences; meaningful ordinary life; persistent agents; and compression that preserves agency, relationships and earned experience. In the recovered August 30 notes, the user explicitly objected to simply skipping boring parts.

These are recovered conversation notes, not a complete original transcript. The “Experience OS,” LIVE/LEARN/LAB and training-workbench framings were later assistant proposals. They must not be treated as the founder's original requirements. Cognify-inspired subjective-time ideas are a speculative horizon, not a capability or a scheduled dependency for this build.

### What changes now

1. **The company track becomes experience-led.** A visitor chooses a world, enters a situation, develops a personal history and returns to consequences. Research and authoring are available through separate entrances.
2. **The research track becomes diagnostic before inventive.** Establish where memory or planning can alter a consequential decision before building another architecture or running a larger sweep.
3. **The common object is a real, replayable life.** Product, debrief, research and optional model interfaces consume the same accepted history through different visibility projections.
4. **Ordinary life is part of the design.** Keep selected routines, quiet interaction and anticipation. Do not optimize the experience into a stream of crises, rewards and compressed summaries.
5. **No generality theatre.** The current compiler supports two finite adapters with fixed rules. Better authoring means making those boundaries usable, not pretending arbitrary prose can create executable societies.

## 3. Strategic diagnosis

**There are two separate discontinuities.**

The human experience is discontinuous when visitors must select an engine mode, interpret numeric receipts, repeatedly confirm trivial actions, reload a scene, or read research language to understand their own situation.

Agent cognition is discontinuous when an added memory or plan object does not influence a later decision, or the evaluation never requires its extra capability. A new class and a trace are not an outcome improvement.

Both problems are real; they require different evidence. Attractive screenshots cannot establish agency. Unit tests cannot establish attachment. A live language model cannot repair a confusing information architecture by itself.

## 5. Company track versus research track

### Company/product track

Question: **Does someone understand, care about and voluntarily revisit a history they helped create?**

Next proof: a coherent public entrance, saved-world home, usable experience screen, readable personal history, and an honest return sequence. A separate commercial challenger tests whether authors or facilitators value trustworthy scenario construction and debriefing. Do not silently convert the company to enterprise training because the workbench is easier to describe.

The company thesis recommends testing both demand hypotheses with small, explicit experiments, not building two startups simultaneously. No pricing, customer count, market-size or valuation conclusion follows from the current build.

### Research track

Question: **Under matched resource and information budgets, what retained evidence is necessary to carry a plan or commitment correctly across an interruption and a change in circumstances?**

Next proof: a necessity/headroom audit, a frozen multi-session task set, credible memory baselines, one small candidate change, actual executed decisions and an honest cost/outcome comparison. Human pacing and learning are separate studies, not extra claims attached to a machine benchmark.

### Shared contract

Both tracks use canonical receipts, dated evidence, participant-visible histories, explicit time semantics, genuine alternative executions and versioned saves. Product code must not silently change frozen benchmark behavior. Research variants must not silently alter user lives.

## 6. Scope and resource choices

Prioritize effort approximately 60% on the working experience, 30% on the discriminating cognition study, and 10% on integration/evidence. These are allocation preferences, not a runtime estimate.

**Not in this release:** a new renderer, larger population, another city, multiplayer, a marketplace, unrestricted world generation, new neural/VR work, a new API transport, a fresh company manifesto, or a new cognition version number without measured behavioral change.

**AI:** use the existing provider interface. Default external spend remains zero unless an authorized credential source and an explicit numeric cap are configured. A local endpoint is usable only if actually available and approved. Without either, ship the deterministic path and mark live-model quality untested. Do not spend the session rebuilding adapters that already exist.

**Money/public actions:** this brief does not authorize purchases, account creation, outreach, recruitment, public deployment, repository publication or unbounded model calls.

## 7. Decision gates

- **Gate A — experience:** a new visitor can enter a world, understand a person-specific situation, make a supported choice, and recognize one actual consequence without seeing LAB.
- **Gate B — continuity:** resume preserves world identity, scene and drafts; a return summary makes no invented or private claims; choices are persisted before being displayed as saved.
- **Gate C — cognition:** the evaluation contains cases where admissible remembered evidence can improve a decision. Otherwise repair the evaluation before changing memory methods.
- **Gate D — intelligence:** a candidate change improves a prespecified consequential endpoint or lowers cost at a prespecified acceptable outcome level. Ties and regressions remain reportable results.
- **Gate E — human value:** formative tests identify whether the experience is understood and worth continuing. They do not establish statistical retention or learning efficacy.

An honest failed gate is useful. Relabelling it as success because more code exists is not.

## 8. Review outcome

This brief rejects four tempting but weak responses: a beautiful marketing-only shell, another generalized OS rewrite, a large LLM-agent deployment before a small live test, and millions of easy benchmark decisions. The chosen replacement is a persistent human journey and a falsifiable research mechanism, joined by the existing world engine.

### Primary external sources checked 24 October 2026

These summaries are deliberately narrow. A verified source is not a replication by Kalends.

- **R1 — MemoryArena**, version 2, 17 September 2026. Multi-session dependent tasks evaluate memory through later action; added memory is not universally helpful. Implication: evaluate carried state in an actual action loop. <https://arxiv.org/html/2602.16313v2>
- **R2 — AMA-Bench**, version 4, 27 May 2026. Agent trajectories, causal-memory retrieval and additional downstream execution analyses. Implication: “memory influences action” is not a new contribution by itself. <https://arxiv.org/html/2602.22769v4>
- **R3 — Mem2ActBench**, version 1, 13 January 2026. Tests applying remembered information to tool selection and argument grounding. Implication: correct recipients, dates, quantities and constraints deserve separate scoring. <https://arxiv.org/html/2601.19935v1>
- **R4 — Hindsight**, original paper, December 2025. Structured memory separates several evidence/experience/belief representations and supports retain/recall/reflect. Implication: do not present this decomposition as a new invention. <https://arxiv.org/html/2512.12818v1>
- **R5 — Can Agent Memory Systems Track Evolving State?**, version 1, 20 August 2026; preprint. Tests current versus superseded state and proposes explicit state tracking. Implication: retrieval success and current-state correctness are different. <https://arxiv.org/html/2608.19652v1>
- **R6 — CounterMem**, version 1, 25 September 2026; preprint. Studies executable verification and conditional reuse of alternative-action memories. Implication: executed counterfactual memory already has close prior work; applicability matters. <https://arxiv.org/html/2609.31874v1>
- **R7 — Generative Agents**, 2023. Model-driven memory, reflection and planning in an interactive society. Implication: believable persistent characters are established research territory, not novelty from a new Roman setting. <https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03442>
- **H1 — Microsoft HAX Guidelines.** Supports clear capability communication, user control, correction and explanations. These are design constraints, not evidence that this specific product will be enjoyable. <https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/ai-guidelines/>
- **H2 — Flores et al., 2017**, primary experiments on event segmentation and delayed event memory. Motivates testing meaningful event boundaries; does not validate Kalends, accelerated learning or subjective-time expansion. <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5542882/>
- **W1 — W3C WCAG 2.2**, Recommendation 12 December 2024. Accessibility acceptance criteria for the web shell and controls. <https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/>
- **W2 — web.dev, Interaction to Next Paint**, updated 2 September 2025. Distinguishes interaction feedback from complete asynchronous work. Do not confuse INP with physical controller latency or game FPS. <https://web.dev/articles/inp>
- **A1 — OpenAI Structured Outputs.** Schema adherence does not guarantee the values or claims are correct. Semantic validation remains necessary. <https://openai.com/index/introducing-structured-outputs-in-the-api/> and <https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/structured-outputs>
- **C1 — ink, official project.** An established authored-narrative tool; evaluate only as an optional content-authoring layer, not a replacement world-state authority. <https://www.inklestudios.com/ink/>
- **C2 — Convai, official PlayCanvas integration article**, 6 November 2024. Conversational 3D characters are an existing product category. Kalends must prove a more specific continuity/experience advantage rather than claim that combination is unique. <https://convai.com/blog/ai-characters-web-browser-playcanvas>

## 11. Final specification review

The completed pack was checked against the following failure modes before delivery:

- **Marketing-only redesign:** prevented by requiring the saved-life journey and committed-state reconciliation before the homepage is treated as complete.
- **Another quiet pivot to training software:** avoided by keeping inhabited curated worlds as the primary product hypothesis and the workbench as a separately tested commercial challenger.
- **Pacing that leaks hidden truth:** prevented by participant-visible stop triggers and explicit distinction between simulation and presentation.
- **A budget advantage disguised as smarter memory:** addressed by charging plan registers, summaries, indexes, embeddings and retrieval/inference costs.
- **Narrative grounded only in canonical history:** prevented by participant/agent visibility and retention boundaries, including the forgotten-episode/persistent-obligation case.
- **A polished but unmeasured model integration:** addressed by separating mock contracts, real provider execution, semantic correctness and expression quality.
- **Unsupported source/API claims:** recommendations are linked to primary sources; the implementation agent must still inspect current interfaces and exact available versions.

The proposed palette's default body, muted, button, accent and night text pairs were checked computationally for contrast. This checks those color pairs only; it is not a website accessibility audit. The current website itself was not rendered or modified here.
