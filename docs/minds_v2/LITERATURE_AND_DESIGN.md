# Minds V2: primary-source comparison and study design

21 October 2026. This is a literature audit and proposed evaluation protocol, not a preregistration or experimental result. The four references supplied in the brief resolve to matching papers. PMLR identifies the first three as ICML 2026 proceedings papers; *Beyond Memory* is an October 2026 preprint, not an established peer-reviewed result. Full methods were inspected through the linked arXiv versions after the PMLR PDF endpoint returned an unsupported content type. No paid model, embedding download or participant study was used.

## What the closest work actually evaluates

**MemoryArena — He et al.** The proceedings title is *Benchmarking Agent Memory in Interdependent Multi-Session Agentic Tasks*. Sections 3–4 construct interdependent shopping, search, travel and formal-reasoning tasks and measure subtask progress, constraint satisfaction and complete-task success. Different memory systems use a common task agent. Appendix C.3 supplies approximate golden prior outcomes/workflows; even this does not remove acting failures. Memory-to-action evaluation is therefore prior art, and memory quality must be separated from planning/execution quality. [Proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/he26am.html), [methods and appendix, arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.16313v2).

**AMA-Bench / AMA-Agent — Zhao et al.** Sections 3 and6 evaluate real agent trajectories and scalable synthetic environments through grounded questions, including temporal, causal, update and abstraction capabilities. Section 5 parses adjacent observation/action/observation triples into state nodes and causal/association edges; retrieval combines embeddings, graph traversal and keyword tools. This is considerably more than plain semantic search, but its reported QA endpoint is different from executing a later bargain under resource constraints. Its graph edges are inferred from trajectories; they are not automatically validated causal interventions. [Proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/zhao26bs.html), [methods, arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.22769v2).

**MEMO — Xie et al.** Sections 3.1–3.3 couple self-play prompt/context evolution, conservative TrueSkill selection, persistent typed trajectory insights and prioritized replay. The memory bank adds, merges and removes insights across optimization generations. The study concerns game outcomes and variance, not just recall. Because prompt policy changes alongside memory, reproducing this whole procedure would not isolate the effect of Kalends memory under a fixed planner. The preprint and proceedings differ in some headline numbers; this audit does not mix their numeric results. [Proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/xie26x.html), [methods, arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.09022v2).

**Beyond Memory / PoS — Luo et al.** Submitted1October 2026. Section 3 maintains an entity/state/relation estimate, task goal, epistemic gaps and achievement gaps. A separate sentinel validates candidate updates; progress stagnation, recurrence and persistent gaps trigger recovery constraints. Section 4 and appendices C–G compare execution and diagnosis tasks with shared backbones, ablate validation/recovery and acknowledge maintenance cost. Explicit beliefs, validated revision and goal-progress monitoring are consequently direct prior art. Kalends cannot claim a new general belief-state architecture from implementing deterministic equivalents. [Versioned full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.01415v1), [submission record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01415).

## Overlap and possible distinction

The last column is a hypothesis or engineering distinction to test, not an established novelty claim.

| Primary work | Exact overlap | Potential Kalends distinction and remaining threat |
|---|---|---|
| [Generative Agents, Park et al., 2023, §§4,6–7](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.03442v2) | Experience stream; retrieval by recency, importance and relevance; evidence-linked reflections; revisable plans; social information spread. | Machine-validated economic execution and per-agent access constraints could support a different evaluation. Authored utility rules alone do not establish believable people; their human believability evaluation is not interchangeable with Kalends invariant checks. |
| [MemoryArena](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.16313v2) | Interdependent sessions, experience reuse and downstream task success. | Multiple partially informed agents and explicit shared-source ancestry may be useful dimensions. Six synthetic families cannot establish broad real-world task generalization. |
| [AMA-Bench / AMA-Agent](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.22769v2) | Temporal updates, causal dependencies, structured memory and mixed retrieval. | Execute resource-changing decisions and test controlled information interventions. A scenario generator and evaluator sharing the same schema can create a trivial structured advantage. |
| [MEMO](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.09022v2) | Consolidated experience influencing later strategic play and measured outcomes. | A fixed selector makes memory ablations easier to interpret. It also excludes MEMO's learned strategic policy improvement, so no superiority claim is justified. |
| [PoS](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.01415v1) | Explicit revisable state, goal-relative gaps, consistency validation and recovery. | Explicit observation permissions and source correlation can be tested in a persistent multi-agent economy. Small deterministic schemas do not demonstrate open-language state maintenance. |
| [Kaelbling, Littman and Cassandra, 1998, §§3–4](https://people.csail.mit.edu/lpk/papers/aij98-pomdp.pdf) | A belief state supports action under partial observation and updates after action/observation. | Kalends' heuristic confidence and bounded records are not a calibrated posterior or a sufficient statistic; POMDP optimality guarantees do not transfer. |
| [McClelland, McNaughton and O'Reilly, 1995](https://stanford.edu/~jlmcc/papers/McCMcNaughtonOReilly95.pdf) | Distinguishing rapid experience storage from slower integrated knowledge is an old consolidation idea. | Symbolic summaries with source pointers are an engineering policy, not a reproduction or validation of complementary biological learning systems. |
| [Erol, Hendler and Nau, 1994](https://drum.lib.umd.edu/items/de8aba70-7f9a-4e32-bf8a-31b6a8f4a5a5) | Hierarchical decomposition and explicit task-network constraints are established planning methods. | Bounded utility selection or a few authored subtasks must be labeled as such; richer goal fields do not by themselves implement general HTN planning. |
| [Bikhchandani, Hirshleifer and Welch, 1992](https://snap.stanford.edu/class/cs224w-readings/bikhchandani92fads.pdf) | Later agents' behavior can reflect earlier agents' information rather than independent evidence. | A relay count is not an independent-source count. The classic model observes predecessor actions, whereas Kalends may transmit explicit statements/root claims; those mechanisms must not be conflated. |

The strongest defensible research direction is **outcome-level coherence under bounded, legitimately observed, revisable and socially correlated information**. No reviewed source establishes that Kalends already improves that endpoint. A Roman setting is not methodological novelty.

## Existing Kalends hazards to avoid repeating

The existing [methodology audit](../../METHODOLOGY_AUDIT.md) already identifies keyed-query structured ceilings, unequal retrieval/table budgets and shared generation grammar. `StructuredMemory` stores every keyed exposure and chooses the latest accepted vintage; `HybridGuardedMemory` prevents a keyed miss from being filled with nearby text. These are useful baselines, not newly invented V2 capabilities. An adapted bounded version must be labeled separately from the unchanged original algorithm.

`kalends/memory/vector.py` uses hashed bag-of-words. It is a lexical similarity diagnostic, not a competitive modern dense baseline. The bundled Python has no installed `sentence_transformers`, `transformers`, `torch`, `mlx` or `onnxruntime`; known Hugging Face and sentence-transformer cache roots were absent. The only discovered Ollama manifest is `qwen3:4b`, a chat model, with no verified embedding model. A real dense baseline is therefore **unavailable in this audit**, not a measured loser. No weights were downloaded and no local or external inference call was made. Record any later availability change separately.

## Proposed consequential evaluation

Freeze a versioned registration, source fingerprint, budgets, reward definitions, development seeds and unused heldout seeds **before** reading heldout effects. The following is design advice for the implementation owner; it is not a claim that these exact cells ran.

**Intervention.** Hold the legal-action validator, utility selector, goals, resource model and observation schedule fixed. Swap only observation encoding/retention, retrieval and belief maintenance. An observation-only contract is the sole input: no world snapshot, evaluator answer, hidden root signature or future event. Give every arm the same communicated source lineage, participant identity, event vintage, receipt time and source uncertainty. A communicated root is a claim unless independently authenticated.

**Arms.** Include FIFO/latest-matching, BM25 with a declared temporal readout, current structured and guarded policies under an explicitly reported bounded adaptation, V2, and V2 without consolidation, source lineage or active revision. Use identical update opportunities and action context limits. Report an unlimited-observed-history reference separately from an omniscient-world upper bound; neither is a deployable budget-matched method. Keep memory ablations separate from a later planner ablation. Do not silently replace a weak baseline after viewing heldout outcomes.

**Budget.** Cap total retained information, not only the visible episode list. Count semantic/support/contradiction/social records, summarized evidence references and any learned preferences; report serialized UTF-8 bytes and peak allocation in addition to slots. Retrieval context has its own shared cap. An unbounded semantic dictionary behind a 32-episode list is an unfair hidden archive. Consolidation may retain a summary and source identifiers while discarding full episodes, but cannot use those IDs to fetch forgotten content during a decision. Report each arm's actual storage and context use rather than assuming nominal caps imply equality.

| Family | Executed consequence | Necessary counterconditions |
|---|---|---|
| Temporal correction | Book/buy from a supplier before a deadline; accepted payment and delivered goods determine utility. | Include unchanged facts, delayed old relays and corrections received too late. A hidden change is not a memory error. |
| Correlated testimony | Extend funded credit or pay for verification using testimony with disclosed ancestry. | Balance truthful and mistaken shared roots against independent evidence. Repeating a correct root can still produce the best outcome. |
| Personal history | Honor, refuse or collateralize a new obligation based on retained fulfilled/broken promises. | Include recovery after a breach and misleading second-hand accusations; permanent distrust must sometimes lose utility. |
| Strategic communication | Share, withhold or purchase a report when disclosure affects both parties' resources/opportunities. | Make verification costly and sometimes unnecessary; indiscriminate abstention or honesty must not be an automatic winning policy. |
| Long absence | Resume an arrangement after routine events and actual state changes; settle the account. | Vary interference length and obsolete high-salience lessons, including cases favoring recent evidence. |
| Goal revision | Replace an infeasible plan after a capacity, resource or deadline change and complete an alternative. | Include stable plans and costly needless replanning, plus cases with no feasible recovery. |

**Endpoint and evaluation.** Primary endpoints are terminal net resource utility and deadline goal completion, computed from actual validated execution/settlement, with all costs included. State action units and reward ranges before aggregation; do not average unrelated raw coin/penalty scales. Report observed-information regret separately from realized-world utility: a faithful agent can be unlucky, and an uninformed guess can be right. The evaluator may inspect world truth, but the agent may not. Check evaluator examples and invariants independently of the memory module.

**Diagnostics.** Record stale-belief persistence and number of legitimate corrective observations until recovery; unnecessary verification/abstention cost; independent-source attribution; access leakage; chosen-action differences under the same information; belief/action consistency; retrieval/plan latency; memory bytes and actual model cost. Classify failures as missing observation, encoding, retention, retrieval, revision, planning, validation/execution or evaluator error. A citation ID alone does not prove that the cited content was available.

**Matched units.** Pair identical initial worlds and exogenous randomness across arms. If actions create different future observations, disclose that dynamic divergence and retain a separate fixed-stream diagnostic instead of pretending later inputs remain equal. Aggregate each seed across its family variants before bootstrap resampling; episodes, decisions and multiple questions are not independent seeds. Report per-family paired differences and uncertainty, not just a pooled rank. Include error bars and raw failures even if V2 loses. Separate paraphrased heldout renderings from the developer grammar, and keep the game demonstration outside confirmatory seeds.

The visible game integration should show a real memory-conditioned allocation, purchase, credit or recovery decision with inspectable supporting experience, belief and plan. Such a demonstration establishes integration. A fixed-policy synthetic experiment can establish a narrow engineering effect; neither establishes general intelligence, human enjoyment or novel memory theory.
