## Skeptical AI researcher

Authoritative accounts, goods, deadlines and ledger events constrain both authored choices and typed proposals. NPC procurement chooses between work, verification, deferral, buying and waiting for a sufficiently reliable promise using owned resources and retained information. This is bounded state-dependent policy, not unrestricted human cognition. Credibility and reliability serve different decisions; competence is presently descriptive.

The provider framework has six structured task contracts, deterministic offline/mock behavior and a real-provider transport path. Intent and dialogue are integrated at the player boundary. Planning, reflection, montage and content interfaces are foundations; their existence does not establish an autonomous LLM society. No live-model quality has been measured and no paid game-inference call was made.

This repairs a concrete case where a retained fulfilled commission was hidden by older records' storage order. It does not expand the retained-memory budget or permit archival retrieval of forgotten events.

The study establishes cheaper isolated transactions after finite histories, with exact declared equivalence; historical writes were slower. Preserve the two ENOSPC failures and the finite-workload uncertainty.
